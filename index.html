Welcome to Beycan Summer Camp

Beycan Future Skills & Technical Training Institute presents an exciting Summer Tech Camp designed for students aged 5+. Our programs focus on developing creativity, logical thinking, and future-ready technical skills through hands-on learning and real-time projects.

With expert trainers and a supportive environment, we ensure every student gains practical knowledge, confidence, and a strong foundation in coding, robotics, and emerging technologies.

Each course is conducted for 2 hours per day. Duration may vary depending on the selected course and level.

Students get FREE Spoken English, Group Discussion, Certificate, and practical projects along with the course.

How can I register for the camp?

You can register through our website, scan the QR code, or contact us directly via phone or WhatsApp.
