FREQUESNTLY ASK QUESTIONS

Build Your Child’s Future with Smart Tech Skills 🚀

What is the course duration?

Each course is conducted for 2 hours per day. Duration may vary depending on the selected course and level.

What courses are included in the camp?

The camp includes Programming (C, Python), Robotics (Arduino), Web Design, Tally, Abacus, and more.

How can I register for the camp?

You can register through our website, scan the QR code, or contact us directly via phone or WhatsApp.

Are there any offers available?

Yes! We offer a free school bag and up to 10% discount for the first 100 students. Limited seats available.
